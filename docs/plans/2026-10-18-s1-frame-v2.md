## T2: frame v2 and writer fields

- Header: `crc | len | kind | version=2 | flags=0 | txn | lsn | commit_time_ns | tenant_id`, 40 bytes. CRC32C covers `segment_id_le || offset_le || bytes[4..len)`. `segment_id` comes from the manifest.
- Remove `tenant_prev_chain`, `content_digest`, salts, `chain_next`, `canon`, `batch_digest`, per-tenant chain state, chain checks, and the caller-side salt and digest work. Keep BLAKE3 only for the dedup digest and checkpoint manifest digests.
- Footer payload: `txn | first_lsn | synced_lsn | record_count | records_crc` (32 bytes). `records_crc` is CRC32C over the concatenated 4-byte record CRCs. `synced_lsn` is the LSN the writer has actually synced when it builds the footer.
- LSN assignment: record frames take one LSN each, dense. Footers take none; footer header LSN is `first_lsn + record_count - 1`. Txns dense. `TenantCreate` takes one LSN; its `tenant_id` is its own id.
- `commit_time = max(previous + 1 ns, clock)`, with `previous` recovered from the tape at open.
- Store the durable LSN after the state machine's publish.
- v1 frames are rejected with a clear error.

## T3: recovery

- Checks for every transaction: dense record LSNs and txns, footer `first_lsn` and `record_count` match, `records_crc` matches, `previous synced_lsn <= synced_lsn < first_lsn`.
- Below the superblock: unchanged (any bad frame, early end, missing segment is `CorruptionDetected`).
- Above the superblock, a bad frame triggers lookahead. Let `d` be the first LSN recovery would drop:
  - Scan forward byte by byte through the rest of the segment and every later manifest segment, skipping zero runs cheaply.
  - Validate whole candidate transactions: location-seeded CRCs, dense LSNs, `records_crc`, header fields and footer guards.
  - Candidates need `first_lsn >= d`. Any validated footer with `synced_lsn >= d` means `CorruptionDetected`.
  - `d` is the last retained transaction's LSN plus 1. Reported bad-frame LSNs are diagnostic only.
  - Otherwise truncate, zero and sync as today. Put the truncated LSN range and byte count in the recovery report.
- `Tape::recover_truncate_at(dir, lsn)`: explicit operator escape that truncates the log after `lsn` and logs it. The `zerodb recover --truncate-at` CLI wiring ships with the binary in S3.

## T4: paged LSN index

- Replace the tape's `BTreeMap<lsn, (segment, offset)>` with per-segment arrays of offsets indexed by `lsn - segment_first_lsn`, paged with a memory budget (I12). Lookup stays O(1).
- Tests: lookup across segments and after recovery, budget respected, reload of evicted pages.

## T5: tests, sims and numbers

- New regression tests:
  - a stale footer from an earlier, longer transaction in a reused region does not validate;
  - a bit flip above the superblock with a later proving footer (same segment and a later segment) gives `CorruptionDetected`;
  - a bit flip above the superblock with no proving footer truncates and reports;
  - a corrupt `len` field;
  - steps that sync without writing (dedup replies only) never cause a false alarm;
  - reordered writeback within one sync group never causes a false alarm;
  - `commit_time` stays monotonic across a restart with a clock that goes backwards.
- Tape and engine sims: add the new fault cases, keep all existing invariants. 2,000 seeds each in CI style, 10,000 once locally.
- G1: 192k turns, 3 interleaved repetitions, engine/bare and tape/bare p99 median and spread. S1 must not be worse than `bench/RESULTS.md` M2 numbers. Update RESULTS with an S1 section, including the smaller header.
