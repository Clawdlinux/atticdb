# LoCoMo retrieval: what the 2026-11-13 run says

Raw tables are in `2026-11-13-summary.md`. Per-question rows (`results.csv`) stay out of the repo. They hold question words from a CC BY-NC dataset.

## Setup

- Same dataset, questions, stores and arms 1 to 5 as the 2026-11-07 run. 1,536 questions are scored.
- New arm 6, `atticdb-ranked`: the whole question as it is, sent with `order=relevance` and `limit` 100.
  - The server keeps turns holding at least 1 query word.
  - It ranks them by the summed IDF of the words they hold. Ties go newest first.
  - The arm and the query rules were fixed in `docs/plans/2026-11-13-search-ranking.md` before this run.
- The binary is `cargo build --release --locked -p atticdb` at commit eacd9c8 (#199). The run used 02f4467, which a rebase replaced. Their source differs only in 2 test files.
- 4 runs, seed 0, on the same Apple M4 laptop as 2026-11-07. The load average was 3 to 6.
- The summary file is the first run. Arms 1 to 5 gave identical rows in all 4 runs.
- Their recall, hit and tokens match 2026-11-07 exactly. So the change left newest order alone.

## Acceptance for #199

- **Recall@10 at least `fts5-bm25`'s: met.** Ranked is 59.5%, BM25 is 56.1%. That is 3.4 points higher.
- **p50 under 1 ms: met.** Ranked p50 is 0.52 ms over the Unix socket. Over 4 runs it was 0.52 to 0.55 ms.
- Nothing was tuned to reach either. The arm, stopwords and query rules were fixed before the run.

## Latency of the ranked arm

| run | searches | p50 | p95 | max | over 1 ms |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1, the summary file | 1536 | 0.52 | 0.70 | 3.73 | 39 |
| 4 runs, range | 1536 each | 0.52 to 0.55 | 0.64 to 0.94 | 3.17 to 3.73 | 27 to 61 |

All times are in ms. Ranked p50 is 4 to 5 times the newest-order arms' p50.

Worst cases were timed apart from the run. Each store was loaded and left 3 s for its index to catch up. Each query then ran 200 times.

- **The run's 10 slowest ranked questions.** In the run they took 1.6 to 3.7 ms.
  - Re-timed, their p50s were 0.47 to 0.70 ms over 2 passes.
  - 6 of the 10 were among the first 5 questions of their conversation.
  - Those ran right after ingest.
  - So their run times were mostly cold start and load, not the query.
- **A synthetic all-short-words query, "is it ok to go on".** Every word is under 3 chars.
  - So the server falls back to all 6 words, and scores each one.
  - It matched at least 100 turns in each of the 10 stores.
  - Pooled over 2,000 searches: p50 0.62 ms, p95 1.11 ms, max 5.43 ms.
  - An earlier pass ran at load 8. It gave p50 0.64 ms, p95 2.64 ms and max 17.66 ms.
  - In 1 store in that pass, its p50 reached 1.02 ms.
- Single searches reached 49.9 ms once, at load 5 to 8. Tails here are noisy.

## Run to run variation

- Arm 6 rows aren't identical across runs. 11 to 16 of its 1,536 rows differed from run 1.
- Overall recall at any k moved 0.2 points at most. Recall@10 was 59.5% to 59.6%. Hit@10 was 66.0% each time.
- Against the settled runs below, every changed row is among a conversation's first 6 questions.
- `N` and each word's `df` come from the word index. Right after ingest, it hasn't caught up yet.
- To check, 2 settled runs slept 3 s after each ingest. Their rows were identical to each other.
  - Those runs gave recall@10 59.6% and hit@10 66.0%.
  - That wait is not in the harness. The 2026-11-07 run had none either.

## What this says

- **A pasted question now finds its evidence.** In newest order the same question finds nothing.
  - Ranked, 66% of questions get an evidence turn in the top 10.
- **Ranked search beats BM25 at every k and in every category.** Both use the same stopwords over the same turns.
- **It returns more text.** Its top 10 average 438 tokens, against BM25's 315.
  - Summed IDF has no length normalization, unlike BM25. That likely favors longer turns.
- **It matches the offline simulation.** The plan's simulation gave 59.5% recall and 66.0% hit at k = 10.
  - The real run gives the same, with the digit-token rule added since.
- **It is slower than newest order but under 1 ms.** p50 rose from about 0.1 ms to 0.52 ms.

## What this doesn't cover

- QA accuracy. A model might recover from a near-miss turn, or fail despite the right one.
- Larger stores. LoCoMo stores hold 369 to 689 turns. Scoring common words costs more as a store grows.
- MCP. `memory_search` ranks by default there. This run goes over HTTP with `order=relevance`.
- Semantic search. Embeddings are #117.
